# TOGAF Standard, 10th Edition — Step 1 Revision Sheet

## ADM as a decision-making process

**Preliminary → A → B → C → D → E → F → G → H → repeat**

**Requirements Management** operates throughout the ADM.

| Phase | Question to recognize |
|---|---|
| Preliminary | How will we establish and organize the architecture capability? |
| A — Architecture Vision | What are we trying to achieve, why, for whom, and within what scope? |
| B — Business Architecture | How must the business operate? |
| C — Information Systems Architectures | What Data and Application Architectures are needed? |
| D — Technology Architecture | What technology capabilities are needed? |
| E — Opportunities & Solutions | How should the required change be packaged? |
| F — Migration Planning | In what priority and sequence should it be implemented? |
| G — Implementation Governance | Is implementation conforming to the approved architecture? |
| H — Architecture Change Management | Does the architecture need to change? |

## Exam-recognition patterns

### Phase A
Choose **Architecture Vision** when the scenario emphasizes:

- stakeholders and their concerns
- scope of the architecture initiative
- business goals, value, and motivation
- a high-level target vision
- authorization and agreement to proceed

**Trigger:** stakeholders + concerns + scope + high-level value/target → **Phase A**.

### Domain-to-phase mapping

- Business processes and capabilities → **B**
- Data → **C**
- Applications → **C**
- Technology and infrastructure → **D**

## Common trap

Do not jump from a business request directly to products, servers, APIs, or cloud platforms. First identify the ADM question and architecture domain being addressed.

## Minimum recall

> **A = Vision, B = Business, C = Data + Applications, D = Technology.**
